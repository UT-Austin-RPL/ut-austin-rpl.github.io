Flora annotates manipulation videos with captions that identify when an action happens. These labels help researchers filter and balance training data. It also scores progress during a task and evaluates the outcome once an episode ends. The team used Flora in the GR00T training pipeline and released the models and code for researchers working with their own robot and human demonstration videos.
